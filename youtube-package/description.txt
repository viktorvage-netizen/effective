In 1964, a 17-year-old stayed awake for 264 hours straight while scientists watched. Here's what 11 days without sleep would do to YOUR body and brain, day by day.

By day 4 he thought a street sign was a person. By day 11 he couldn't count backward from 100. And what happened when he finally slept is the strangest part of all.
