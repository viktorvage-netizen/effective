## 1. Video file specs

- **Format:** MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio
- **Resolution:** 1920×1080 (1080p), 16:9
- **Frame rate:** 24, 25 or 30 fps (keep it the same for the whole video)
- **Length:** about 10:00. The narration is 1,495 words, which is 10 minutes at 150 words per minute.
- **File name:** `what-if-you-stayed-awake-11-days.mp4`. A descriptive name is a small extra signal; `final_v3.mp4` is not.

## 3. Thumbnails

All three are 1280×720 PNG and under 2 MB, which is what YouTube requires.

| | Idea | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **A** (main) | Exhausted cartoon face, bloodshot eyes, coffee, melting clock, **"DAY 11"** | Matches the title exactly. One clear face and one big number. |
| **B** | **DAY 1 vs DAY 11** before-and-after split | Before-and-after is one of the most-clicked thumbnail formats. Shows the change at a glance. |
| **C** | A street sign with a face, circled in red: **"IS HE REAL?"** | Curiosity. Teases the hallucination story from the middle of the video. |

**How to A/B test:** in YouTube Studio on a computer, open the video → **Thumbnail** → **Test & compare**, and upload all three. YouTube shows each to part of your audience and keeps the one with the most watch time. If your channel doesn't have the feature yet, start with **A**, and switch to **B** if the click-through rate is under about 4% after 48 hours.

**Check before uploading:** shrink the image to the size of a phone thumbnail (around 160 px wide). The face and the text should still be readable. All three pass this check.

## 4. Description

Paste `description.txt` as it is. It includes:
- The two hook lines that show before "…more"
- **Chapters.** These become clickable sections on the video timeline.
- A comment question, like and subscribe lines, and a "not medical advice" note
- Sources. These build trust on a health topic.
- 3 hashtags (`#sleep #science #whatif`). They show above the title.

⚠️ **Fix the chapter times after editing.** They're calculated from the narration speed. If the final video runs faster or slower, move each time to where that section actually starts. YouTube's rules for chapters: the first one must be `0:00`, there must be at least 3, and each must be at least 10 seconds long.

## 5. Tags

Paste `tags.txt` (423 of the 500 allowed characters). Tags matter less than the title and thumbnail. They mostly help with misspellings and related searches.

## 10. When to publish

- Schedule it instead of publishing right away. That gives YouTube time to finish the HD version so the video goes live in 1080p from the first minute.
- **New channel:** pick a weekday, 2–3 hours before the evening peak in your main audience's time zone (for a US audience, about 12–3 PM Eastern).
- **Once you have viewers:** go to **Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube"** and publish just before the darkest bars.
- Stick to the same day and time each week. Consistency matters more than the exact hour.

## 13. Monetization notes for a faceless channel

To get into the YouTube Partner Program you need 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public watch hours in a year or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.

YouTube also checks for "inauthentic", mass-produced content. Protect the channel by keeping:
- **Original scripts,** like this one (researched and sourced, not copied from another channel)
- **One consistent narrator voice and art style** from video to video
- **Real editing:** pacing, sound effects, jokes and pattern interrupts, not a slideshow over text-to-speech
- **Sources in the description**
