## Batching plan

Week 1: write all 10 scripts.
Week 2: record all 10 VOs in one session (same mic, same room, same energy = consistent channel voice).
Week 3: build one editing template, then assemble 10 videos.
Then publish 3× per week and keep the pipeline 3 weeks ahead permanently.

## Rules that stay fixed across every episode

1. Hook in the first 10 seconds, with an open loop you pay off at 70–80% of the runtime.
2. 30–45 seconds per item. Never longer.
3. Second person throughout — "you", never "people".
4. Thumbnail: 2–4 words, one focal element, readable at phone size.
5. Every factual claim is checkable. The title can be curious; it can never be a lie.
